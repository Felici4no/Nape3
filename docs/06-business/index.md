# Business

This section intentionally remains hypothesis-driven.

## Current product value

The immediate value is reducing search friction and exposing cross-platform price differences.

## Potential customers

Only one should be prioritized at a time.

Possible directions:

- consumers;
- merchants seeking competitive intelligence;
- agent developers needing normalized local-commerce data.

## Potential business models

To validate later:

- consumer premium features;
- referral or transaction revenue;
- merchant analytics;
- API access;
- market-data subscriptions.

## Rule

Do not present a business model as validated until evidence exists that a customer is willing to pay.
