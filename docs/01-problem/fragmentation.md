# Fragmented delivery market

## Hypothesis

The same purchase intent is distributed across multiple isolated marketplaces.

## Example

A user wants 500 ml of açaí.

They may separately search:

- iFood;
- Rappi;
- 99Food;
- a merchant's direct ordering channel.

Each source may present a different:

- product title;
- nominal price;
- promotion;
- delivery fee;
- service fee;
- ETA;
- minimum order.

The research objective is to measure how much information a user must reconcile manually before making a purchase decision.

## Evidence to collect

Real screenshots, timestamps, offers, fees, and final checkout values for equivalent purchase intents.

Do not generalize from a single observation.
