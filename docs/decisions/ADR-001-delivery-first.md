# ADR-001 — Delivery first

## Status

Accepted.

## Context

The product thesis could generalize to many fragmented marketplaces. Generalizing too early would make the hackathon scope unclear.

## Decision

Use food delivery as the initial vertical.

The hackathon MVP will focus on a narrow set of substitutable food categories.

## Consequences

- Domain models may remain generic where inexpensive.
- Product language and demo remain delivery-specific.
- Other verticals stay outside MVP scope.
