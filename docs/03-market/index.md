## Landscape (2026)

- **iFood** is the incumbent. CADE's 2023 settlement limits its exclusivity
  deals ([Tecnoblog](https://tecnoblog.net/noticias/ifood-esta-proibido-de-fechar-exclusividade-com-grandes-redes-decide-cade/)).
- **99Food** (DiDi) relaunched in 2025, with about 70 cities reported in March 2026.
- **Keeta** (Meituan) started in Santos in Oct 2025 and in São Paulo in Dec 2025.
- **Rappi** operates. Uber Eats left restaurant delivery in 2022.

More restaurants listed on more than one app is what makes cross-platform
comparison useful. Market-size numbers are still to be sourced. Sources and
access paths are in [data sources](data-sources.md).
